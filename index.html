The math, made visible

IRIRPRO solves the IRR equation directly — no APR approximations, no rounding tricks.

0 = Σ CFt / (1 + IRR)t

Where CFt is the net cash flow at period t. The Newton-Raphson solver converges to the rate that makes the Net Present Value exactly zero.
