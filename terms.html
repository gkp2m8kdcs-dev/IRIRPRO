4.Calculations & Results

IRIRPRO performs mathematical computations using the values you enter. The Internal Rate of Return is solved with a Newton-Raphson iterative method; amortization schedules use the standard reducing-balance formula; and the Effective Annual Rate (EAR) is derived from the IRR using the standard compounding equation.

While we have designed the engine to be robust — handling edge cases such as fees that consume the principal, zero-rate periods, and irregular schedules — the results are mathematical estimates. They depend entirely on the accuracy and completeness of the numbers you provide. Rounding, fees not captured in the input, taxes, insurance, late charges, balloon payments, variable rates, and unusual repayment schedules can materially affect real-world outcomes.

Always verify the figures with your lender before signing any agreement. IRIRPRO's outputs are educational references, not contractual quotations.
