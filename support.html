Quick Start Guide

① Enter your loan

Open the Calculator tab. Type the principal, term in months, nominal annual rate, and any upfront fees. The Effective Annual Rate updates instantly.

② Read the chart

The payoff curve plots remaining balance over time. Tap any month to inspect principal, interest, and cumulative cost.

③ Save the scenario

Tap "Save" to add this loan to your dashboard. Saved scenarios appear on the home tab and in History for comparison.

④ Compare offers

Save each offer separately, then open Insights to see them side by side — same principal, different terms, the cheaper loan becomes obvious.

⑤ Export & share

From the History tab, share an amortization table as plain text or a CSV file with anyone you choose.

⑥ Wipe everything

Open Settings → Privacy → "Clear All" to permanently remove every stored scenario from your device.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the Effective Annual Rate (EAR) calculated?
IRIRPRO uses a Newton-Raphson solver to find the Internal Rate of Return (IRR) that discounts every cash flow of the loan to a Net Present Value of exactly zero. The IRR is then expressed annually and converted into the EAR using the standard compounding formula: EAR = (1 + periodic)n − 1, where n is the number of compounding periods per year.
Why does my EAR differ from the rate printed on the contract?
The "rate" most lenders publish is the nominal annual rate — it ignores compounding, fees, and timing of payments. IRIRPRO shows the true annualized cost including all of those factors, which is almost always higher than the headline rate.
Does IRIRPRO work offline?
Yes. Every feature — Calculator, History, Dashboard, Insights, Settings — works without an internet connection. The app makes no network requests of its own.
Where is my data stored?
Exclusively on your device, inside the iOS app sandbox. Nothing is synced to iCloud, Google Drive, Dropbox, or any custom server. Removing the app or tapping "Clear All" permanently erases the data.
Can I export my scenarios?
Yes. Open any saved scenario from the History tab and use the share button to export the full amortization schedule as plain text or CSV. The file is produced on-device and shared via the standard iOS share sheet.
What does the risk classification mean?
IRIRPRO tags every saved scenario with a risk band based on its Effective Annual Rate: Excellent (≤ 12%), Fair (12–25%), High (25–45%), Very High (45–80%), Predatory (> 80%). These are educational thresholds, not legal advice.
Is IRIRPRO financial advice?
No. IRIRPRO is an educational calculation tool. It produces mathematical estimates from the values you enter. It is not a substitute for advice from a qualified financial professional. See the Terms of Use for the full disclaimer.
