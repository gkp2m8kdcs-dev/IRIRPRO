Privacy Policy

IRIRPRO is a fully offline calculator. This page explains, in plain language, what we do — and what we don't — with your data.

Last updated: September 1, 2026
The short version: We collect nothing. We have no servers. Your loan scenarios never leave your device.

1. Data We Collect

None. IRIRPRO does not collect, transmit, store, sell, or share any personal data. The app does not request your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any other identifier.

Data you enter stays on your device

Every loan amount, interest rate, term, fee, and scenario you save inside IRIRPRO is stored exclusively in the app's local sandbox on your iPhone or iPad. The data is protected by iOS sandboxing and, optionally, your device passcode and Face ID / Touch ID.

No analytics, no telemetry

IRIRPRO integrates no analytics SDKs, no crash reporters, no advertising networks, no attribution platforms, and no remote logging. There is no A/B testing, no funnel tracking, and no event beacon of any kind.

2. How Your Data Is Stored

All saved scenarios, the calculation history, and your preferences live in a private local store inside the app container. The exact technical implementation uses Apple's standard on-device persistence APIs.

  • Data is encrypted at rest by the iOS file system when the device is locked.
  • Data is not included in iCloud backups created by the app itself (no backup is ever produced).
  • Removing IRIRPRO from your device permanently deletes all stored data.
  • You may wipe every saved scenario at any time using the "Clear All" action in Settings.

3. Network Activity

The app is fully functional offline. The only network request that may occur is the system's standard Apple App Store receipt-validation check, which is initiated by iOS itself — not by IRIRPRO — and contains no user-identifiable information about your loan scenarios.

4. Children's Privacy

IRIRPRO is a general-audience utility and is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no data from anyone, the requirements of the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and equivalent international regulations are inherently satisfied.

5. Third Parties

No third-party SDK, framework, or service receives any information from IRIRPRO. The app is fully self-contained.

6. International Users

Because nothing leaves your device, IRIRPRO does not transfer personal data across borders. The app is fully compliant with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the UK Data Protection Act 2018, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA / CPRA), and Brazil's LGPD.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we ever change this policy in a way that affects data collection, we will update the "Last updated" date above and surface a notice inside the app. Because the policy has always been and remains "we collect nothing," no such change is anticipated.
